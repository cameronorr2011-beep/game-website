What this page does and does not show

  • Each request integrates a fresh network (329 conductance-based neurons, ~4.6k synapses, 10 populations) — nothing is replayed or animated.
  • Requests are stateless and capped at 4 s of biological time, so a full night and its ultradian cycling cannot be shown here. Long runs need an always-on host.
  • Deep slow-wave sleep (N3) is not produced by this circuit and most low-arousal windows classify as N1; REM also rarely certifies because the circuit has no theta-generating pathway, so theta never clearly exceeds delta. Both failures are displayed rather than hidden.
  • Thalamic cells carry a low-threshold Ca²⁺ current; cortical cells are conductance-based integrate-and-fire, not Hodgkin–Huxley.
