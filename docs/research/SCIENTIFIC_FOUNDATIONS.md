# Scientific foundations and inspected sources

Sources inspected during this session, not a claim of exhaustive literature review:

1. FlyWire Consortium v783.0 connectivity deposit, https://zenodo.org/records/10676866, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10676866. Read full release column definitions and API license metadata (CC-BY-4.0). The proofread table aggregates by pre neuron, post neuron and neuropil; it is NOT one row per individual synapse. Dorkenwald et al. Nature 2024 combines segmentation, predicted synapses and neurotransmitter work (Buhmann et al. 2021; Heinrich et al. 2018; Eckstein, Bates et al. 2024). Published anatomy does not supply activity recordings or per-cell membrane parameters.
2. FlyWire annotations, https://github.com/flyconnectome/flywire_annotations. Current annotations differ from publication releases and must be pinned separately. Cell-type/lineage annotations and morphology were investigated but not ingested in this implementation.
3. BANC official paper repository, https://github.com/htem/BANC-project. Official README describes v888 metadata/edgelists, approximately 188,000 neurons and 199 million predicted synapses; these are source-reported, NOT locally imported counts. This is a separate female brain-and-nerve-cord specimen, not a newer interchangeable FAFB snapshot. Keep distinct dataset identity. The reported v888 artifact and current Codex can diverge. Do not merge anatomical identities.
4. HCP Young Adult, https://humanconnectome.org/study/hcp-young-adult. Inspected release text: 2025 reprocessing, 1071 processed subjects, 1113 with unprocessed imaging, MEG/7T subsets. Access agreement still required. No HCP credentials, acceptance or data download was attempted.
5. TVB complete dataset specification, https://docs.thevirtualbrain.org/manuals/UserGuide/Complete_Dataset_Description.html. Matrix/labels consistency, acquisition provenance, tract lengths, observation models and coordinate systems are distinct requirements. TVB dataset source is archived; inspected GPL-3.0-or-later license. Cite Sanz Leon et al. 2013, DOI 10.3389/fninf.2013.00010. Example anatomy is not complete requested coverage.
6. Wilson & Cowan 1972, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/4332108/ — primary reference for coupled excitatory/inhibitory populations. Current implementation is a reduced model family with explicitly exploratory constants, not a paper replication or fitted human model.
7. Fulcher, Phillips, Postnova & Robinson 2014, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3961294/, DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0091982. Read model/physiology context, not just abstract. Important limitation: that model explicitly does NOT distinguish REM and NREM stages. Therefore five-stage support cannot honestly be attributed to it. It motivates mutually inhibitory wake/sleep populations, orexin roles and homeostatic/circadian influences, not the exploratory REM equations implemented here.
