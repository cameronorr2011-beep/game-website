Custom weather & controls
Want a game? Pick Air race. Start airborne after a 3-second countdown. Fly through seven rings in order within 120 seconds. Gold = next ring; cyan = upcoming. Follow the ring altitude with gentle pitch corrections. Races use fixed clear midday weather for comparable times. Best times are saved per plane and runway on this browser. Hangar ends your current flight so you can switch aircraft.

Arrow keys: pitch / roll · W/S: throttle · A/D: rudder · B: brakes · F: flaps · C: camera. Right-drag: look. Click colored cockpit switches or open Aircraft systems.

Recommended first flight: leave Engine running / runway selected, click Enter cockpit, then hold W until the plane accelerates (progress shows in throttle %), pull back gently with ↓ at 50–55 kt, and climb. Sound: click Audio off → Audio on once (browsers require one click). Camera: 1 cockpit, 2 chase, 3 free. Full startup exists under Cold & dark for training: Battery → Ignition → Start.

Aircraft systems

Fixed landing gear. Electrical bus powers cockpit gauges and lights; magneto-like ignition survives bus loss.
