Custom weather & controls
Want a game? Pick Air race or Aerobatics. Start airborne after a 3-second countdown. Fly through seven rings in order within 120 seconds. Gold = next ring; cyan = upcoming. Follow the ring altitude with gentle pitch corrections. Races use fixed clear midday weather for comparable times. Best times are saved per plane and runway on this browser. Hangar ends your current flight so you can switch aircraft.

W: power/forward · S: brake (also air brake) · A/D: bank left/right · Shift/Ctrl: fine power · Q/E: rudder · B/Space: brakes · F: flaps · R: starter · T: smoke · P: autopilot · C: camera · M: full map. Arrow keys: manual pitch/roll. Right-drag: look.

Just play: choose a world and aircraft, leave Easy flight and Already flying selected, then Enter cockpit. W climbs, S dives, A/D turn; let go to fly level. All planes work on all four worlds. Hangar lets you change either. Sound requires clicking Audio on. Manual flight and cold starts remain optional.

Aircraft systems

Fixed landing gear. Electrical bus powers cockpit gauges and lights; magneto-like ignition survives bus loss.
