Custom weather & controls
Want a game? Pick Air race. Start airborne after a 3-second countdown. Fly through seven rings in order within 120 seconds. Gold = next ring; cyan = upcoming. Follow the ring altitude with gentle pitch corrections. Races use fixed clear midday weather for comparable times. Best times are saved per plane and runway on this browser. Hangar ends your current flight so you can switch aircraft.

W/S: climb/dive · A/D: turn · Shift/Ctrl: power · Q/E: rudder · Space/B: brakes · F: flaps · R: starter · C: camera · M: full map. Arrow keys mirror WASD. Right-drag: look.

Just play: choose a world and aircraft, leave Easy flight and Already flying selected, then Enter cockpit. W climbs, S dives, A/D turn; let go to fly level. All three planes work on all four worlds. Hangar lets you change either. Sound requires clicking Audio on. Manual flight and cold starts remain optional.

Aircraft systems

Fixed landing gear. Electrical bus powers cockpit gauges and lights; magneto-like ignition survives bus loss.

World map

    Terrain, coastline, airports, checkpoints and your flight path. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, click to set waypoint coordinates, then Add waypoint. Full world = 50 × 50 km. Changing world/plane ends the current flight.

    Flight controls

    W / S (↑ / ↓)
    Climb / dive. NOT inverted. Release to level in Easy flight.
    A / D (← / →)
    Turn left / right. Release to level the wings.
    Shift / Ctrl
    Increase / decrease throttle
    Q / E
    Rudder / nose-wheel steering
    B (hold)
    Wheel brakes
    F / [ / ]
    Cycle flaps / trim down / trim up
    R
    Starter (requires battery, ignition, fuel & rich mixture)
    C / 1 / 2 / 3
    Cycle / cockpit / chase / free camera
    Right mouse drag / wheel
    Look around / field of view
    Free camera: WASD / QZ
    Move horizontally / up-down (aircraft controls suspended)
    M / Esc / ?
    Map / pause / reference
    Gamepad
    Left stick: roll/pitch · right stick X: rudder · triggers: power · A: brakes

    Easy flight is game-style assisted flight, not a realistic simulator. Already flying is the fastest start. Runway takeoff: hold Shift for power, then W to climb. Landing: Ctrl to reduce power, S to descend gently, release S near the runway, Space to brake. Manual flight disables auto-leveling and retains stalls. Invert pitch applies only to manual flight.
